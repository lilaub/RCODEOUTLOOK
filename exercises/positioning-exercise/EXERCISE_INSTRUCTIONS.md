# Product Positioning Exercise: EnergyBoost Beverage Launch

## Marketing Management - Undergraduate Exercise

---

## Learning Objectives

By completing this exercise, students will be able to:

1. **Analyze** competitive positioning in a real-world market context
2. **Create** perceptual maps to visualize brand positions
3. **Identify** meaningful market segments and target audiences
4. **Develop** effective positioning statements using industry frameworks
5. **Evaluate** positioning strategies and make strategic recommendations

---

## Exercise Overview

**Duration**: 75-90 minutes (can be split into two sessions)
**Format**: Individual or teams of 2-3 students
**Tools Required**: Web browser, included positioning app

---

## Scenario

### Background

You have been hired as a marketing consultant by **VitalWave Beverages**, a mid-sized beverage company known for its successful line of sparkling waters and vitamin-infused drinks. The company has decided to enter the highly competitive **energy drink market** with a new product called **"EnergyBoost."**

The CEO has tasked your team with developing the positioning strategy for EnergyBoost before the product launch in 6 months.

### Market Context

The energy drink market is valued at $86 billion globally and continues to grow at 7% annually. However, the market is crowded with established players and increasingly health-conscious consumers.

### Your Challenge

Develop a clear, differentiated positioning for EnergyBoost that will allow VitalWave to carve out a meaningful space in this competitive market.

---

## The Competitive Landscape

### Major Competitors

| Brand | Key Attributes | Target Audience | Price Point |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Red Bull** | Extreme sports, "gives you wings," premium | Young professionals, athletes, 18-35 | High ($3.50/can) |
| **Monster** | Bold, edgy, gaming culture, large cans | Gamers, extreme sports fans, 16-30 | Medium ($2.75/can) |
| **Rockstar** | Rock lifestyle, party culture | Party-goers, night shift workers, 18-35 | Medium ($2.50/can) |
| **5-Hour Energy** | Functional, no crash, small shots | Busy professionals, 25-55 | High ($3.00/shot) |
| **Celsius** | Fitness-focused, thermogenic, healthy | Fitness enthusiasts, health-conscious, 22-40 | High ($2.75/can) |
| **ZOA** | Clean ingredients, veteran-owned, wellness | Health-conscious athletes, 25-45 | Medium-High ($2.50/can) |
| **Alani Nu** | Low calorie, trendy, Instagram-worthy | Young women, fitness influencers, 18-35 | Medium ($2.50/can) |
| **GHOST** | Gamer-focused, licensed flavors, fun | Gamers, pop culture fans, 16-30 | Medium ($2.75/can) |

### Market Trends

1. **Health & Wellness**: Consumers increasingly seek "better-for-you" options with natural ingredients
2. **Functional Benefits**: Beyond energy, consumers want additional benefits (focus, immunity, etc.)
3. **Sustainability**: Eco-friendly packaging and sustainable sourcing matter to Gen Z
4. **Personalization**: Different energy needs for different occasions (work, workout, gaming)
5. **Sugar Reduction**: Growing demand for zero-sugar or low-sugar options

## Part 1: Competitive Analysis (20 minutes)

### Task 1.1: Perceptual Mapping

Using the **Positioning App**, create a perceptual map of the energy drink market.

**Instructions:**
1. Open the Positioning App in your browser
2. Navigate to the "Perceptual Map" section
3. Select two dimensions that you believe are most important for differentiation
4. Position each competitor brand on the map based on your analysis
5. Identify gaps and opportunities in the market

**Suggested Dimension Pairs to Consider:**
- Price vs. Health Focus
- Traditional vs. Innovative
- Performance-Oriented vs. Lifestyle-Oriented
- Mainstream vs. Niche Appeal
- Functional vs. Experiential

**Deliverable:** Screenshot or export of your completed perceptual map

### Task 1.2: Gap Analysis

Based on your perceptual map, answer the following questions:

1. Which area of the map has the most competition?
2. Where do you see potential "white space" (underserved areas)?
3. Which gap represents the best opportunity for EnergyBoost? Why?

## Part 2: Target Market Selection (15 minutes)

### Task 2.1: Segment Evaluation

VitalWave's research team has identified five potential target segments. Evaluate each using the app's segment evaluator:

| Segment | Description | Size | Growth | Accessibility |
|---------|-------------|------|--------|---------------|
| **Wellness Warriors** | Health-conscious professionals who want clean energy | Large | High | Medium |
| **Productive Parents** | Busy parents juggling work and family | Medium | Medium | High |
| **Sustainable Students** | Eco-conscious college students | Medium | High | Medium |
| **Mindful Athletes** | Performance-focused but ingredient-conscious athletes | Small | High | Low |
| **Remote Workers** | Work-from-home professionals needing focus | Large | High | High |

### Task 2.2: Target Selection

Using the app, evaluate each segment based on:
- **Attractiveness**: Market size, growth potential, profitability
- **Fit**: Alignment with VitalWave's capabilities and brand
- **Accessibility**: Ability to reach and serve this segment effectively

**Deliverable:** Select your primary and secondary target segments with justification

## Part 3: Positioning Development (25 minutes)

### Task 3.1: Points of Parity and Difference

For your chosen target segment, identify:

**Points of Parity (POP)** - What must EnergyBoost have to be considered a legitimate energy drink?
- List 3-4 essential attributes

**Points of Difference (POD)** - What will make EnergyBoost meaningfully different and better?
- List 2-3 differentiating attributes

### Task 3.2: Positioning Statement

Using the app's Positioning Statement Generator, create a positioning statement following this framework:

```
For [TARGET SEGMENT]
Who [STATEMENT OF NEED/OPPORTUNITY]
EnergyBoost is a [PRODUCT CATEGORY]
That [KEY BENEFIT/POINT OF DIFFERENCE]
Unlike [COMPETITIVE ALTERNATIVE]
Our product [PRIMARY REASON TO BELIEVE]
```

**Requirements:**
- Be specific about your target
- Focus on one primary benefit
- Make the competitive frame clear
- Include a credible reason to believe

### Task 3.3: Positioning Evaluation

Evaluate your positioning statement using the D-C-U framework:

| Criterion | Question | Your Rating (1-5) | Justification |
|-----------|----------|-------------------|---------------|
| **Desirable** | Do customers want this benefit? | | |
| **Deliverable** | Can we actually deliver this? | | |
| **Differentiating** | Is this meaningfully different from competitors? | | |

## Instructor Notes

### Facilitation Tips

1. **Before class**: Ensure all students can access the positioning app
2. **During Part 1**: Walk around to see perceptual map choices - discuss interesting dimension selections
3. **Discussion points**:
   - Why do different teams choose different dimensions?
   - Is there a "right" answer for positioning?
   - How do real companies reposition over time?

### Extension Activities

- Have teams pitch against each other as competing agencies
- Assign different segments to different teams and compare outcomes
- Research actual energy drink launches and compare to class recommendations

### Answer Key Considerations

There is no single "correct" positioning for EnergyBoost. Evaluate based on:
- Internal consistency of the strategy
- Realistic assessment of competition
- Clear understanding of the target
- Feasibility of execution
